About Us
Carriers You
Can Count On.

Bombino Machin Corp is a licensed motor carrier headquartered in Hialeah, Florida. We operate as both a common and contract carrier, specializing in the transport of automobiles and motor vehicles across the continental United States.

From individual owners shipping a single vehicle to dealerships managing fleet movements — we deliver with reliability and precision.

We serve individuals relocating across the country, automotive dealerships managing inventory, and commercial clients with recurring transport needs. Our operations are backed by industry-grade logistics platforms and real-time fleet tracking.

Auto Transport

Safe and insured transport of personal and commercial vehicles across all 48 continental US states via open and enclosed carriers.

Dealership Logistics

Dedicated fleet management for automotive dealerships — handling high-volume vehicle transfers, lot-to-lot moves, and auction pickups.

Real-Time Tracking

Clients get live visibility into their shipment status through our integrated freight management platforms — no guessing, ever.

Now Entering the UAE
Taking Our
Operations Global.

Having built a strong operational foundation across the continental United States, Bombino Machin Corp is now extending its transport and logistics operations to the UAE market.

The UAE — and Dubai in particular — represents one of the world's most active markets for vehicle import, luxury auto transport, and dealership logistics. We are positioning to serve this market with the same reliability that defines our US operations.

Vehicle Import Logistics — Supporting individuals and businesses importing vehicles into the UAE market.

Dealership Partnerships — Connecting with UAE automotive dealerships for inventory transport and fleet management.

Luxury & High-Value Auto Transport — Enclosed, premium transport suited to the UAE's premium vehicle market.

US–UAE Corridor — Facilitating vehicle exports from the United States to the UAE and broader GCC region.
