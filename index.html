About Us

Three Divisions.
One Standard.

Bombino Corporation is a Florida-based equipment company committed to bringing engineering excellence to emerging and high-growth markets. What began as an industrial shredding distributor has grown into a diversified equipment group spanning document & data destruction, banking & cash-handling technology, and biometric security systems.

All of our equipment is sourced domestically from trusted U.S. manufacturers, giving clients shorter lead times, straightforward support, and equipment built to U.S. quality standards. Our strategic focus remains the Middle East and Africa — regions where financial institutions, government agencies, and enterprises are modernizing rapidly and need reliable equipment backed by real local support.

Division 01

Shredding & Data Destruction

Document, media, and industrial-grade shredding equipment for secure destruction at any scale — from office shredders to twin-shaft recycling systems.

Division 02

Banking & Cash Handling

Currency counters, sorters, and counterfeit-detection systems built for banks, exchange houses, retail, and cash-in-transit operators.

Division 03

Biometric & Access Control

Facial-recognition time & attendance terminals, access control, and workforce management hardware for offices, factories, and secure sites.
